Containers, logistics and unbeatable prices.

ONEWAY containers (almost-new containers after a single journey) are one of our frequent offers. By using several container terminals in multiple European countries, we can arrange deliveries from all over the world. Our priority source is China, where we buy larger series directly from manufacturers. The ordered container then completes only one ocean voyage, whose freight cost is borne by the cargo owner and thus contributes, albeit slightly, to the price of the container itself. Thanks to sophisticated logistics, an extensive supplier network and modern information systems, we can provide our customers with containers of all types and sizes within a relatively short time, usually in the requested RAL colour, in any quantity and including delivery throughout Europe. Thanks to the unique direct link between manufacturer and end customer, with no storage or only minimal storage time, we can offer ONEWAY containers at unbeatable prices.

ONEWAY containers

Almost-new containers after a single journey

Robust, certified and versatile ISO containers with minimal or no wear.

Shipping containers comply with ISO standards, feature corner castings and offer high durability. They are made from CORTEN steel, a special alloy containing copper, chromium and nickel that forms a protective rust-like layer (patina). This layer significantly slows further corrosion. We offer 20' and 40' sizes, including standard, high-cube (HC) and special variants. They are ideal for long-term storage, logistics and conversions into offices or living spaces.

All containers supplied by us have smoothly operating doors, seals in perfect condition and a strong, clean floor with no visible damage. All have a lock box and a high-quality security lock worth EUR 50 is supplied with every container free of charge.

Used shipping containers

Certified used containers for storage and conversions

Availability is individual and changes continuously. Prices shown are indicative and may vary slightly.

Used shipping containers in our offer are always ISO-certified and structurally fully sound, but they do show signs of use. Dents, rust spots, minor cosmetic defects or properly completed repairs with no impact on functionality may be present. The paint may be faded.

Doors are always correctly adjusted, functional, watertight and properly aligned. The floor may show scuffs or minor damage but remains fully serviceable. The containers are dry and watertight. They are suitable for storage or conversions where appearance is not a priority.

The containers offered are located in various parts of Slovakia, the Czech Republic and Poland, so transport costs are calculated individually. Send us your enquiry and we will try to find a container matching your requirements as close to you as possible.
